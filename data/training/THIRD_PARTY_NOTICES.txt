Project Aghoy training corpus - third-party notices
====================================================

The corpus in this directory is built from the following externally published
datasets. Each is included under its respective permissive license. Aghoy
sanitizes every row through the Rejects PII layer before inclusion.

- Dizzzy0x00 / LLMGen-Phishing-Email-Dataset (Apache-2.0)
  License: apache-2.0
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dizzzy0x00/LLMGen-Phishing-Email-Dataset
  Files:   https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dizzzy0x00/LLMGen-Phishing-Email-Dataset/resolve/main/GPT_Phishing_Email_dataset.csv

- UCI Machine Learning Repository SMS Spam Collection, mirrored by codesignal (CC-BY-4.0)
  License: cc-by-4.0
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/datasets/codesignal/sms-spam-collection
  Files:   https://huggingface.co/datasets/codesignal/sms-spam-collection/resolve/main/sms-spam-collection.csv

- Rehan M. / ScamShield Scam Detection Data (MIT)
  License: mit
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/datasets/rehan-ml/scamshield-scam-detection-data
  Files:   https://huggingface.co/datasets/rehan-ml/scamshield-scam-detection-data/resolve/main/train.csv, https://huggingface.co/datasets/rehan-ml/scamshield-scam-detection-data/resolve/main/test.csv, https://huggingface.co/datasets/rehan-ml/scamshield-scam-detection-data/resolve/main/validation.csv


The remainder of the corpus is Project Aghoy's own Taglish seed corpus and
sanitized community reports (Project Aghoy, see LICENSE in the repo root).
